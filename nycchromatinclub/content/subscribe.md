The club's flyers and posters point here, so this page exists at the address printed on them: **nycchromatinclub.org/subscribe**.

## Join the mailing list

The mailing list is how everything reaches the community first — the symposium date, the abstract deadline, the registration link, and seminar announcements from across the participating institutions.

There is no membership application and no fee. Anyone working on chromatin, epigenetics, or nuclear biology in the New York area is welcome — faculty, postdocs, graduate students, staff scientists, technicians, and undergraduates.

See [Join](/join/) for the Slack forum and the rest of the club's channels.
