        <ol class="ncc-timeline ncc-wide">

          <li>
            <div class="ncc-tl-year">2020</div>
            <div class="ncc-tl-body">
              <h3>Founded, out of necessity</h3>
              <p>COVID closed the campuses and with them every seminar, every joint lab meeting, every hallway conversation. <strong>David Shechter</strong> started the NYC Chromatin Club as a virtual seminar series to keep the city's chromatin labs in contact with each other. It was meant to last as long as the shutdown did.</p>
            </div>
          </li>

          <li>
            <div class="ncc-tl-year">2020&ndash;23</div>
            <div class="ncc-tl-body">
              <h3>It outlived the reason for it</h3>
              <p>The virtual format turned out to solve a problem that predated the pandemic: a postdoc in Manhattan and a graduate student in the Bronx had no standing way to hear each other's work. The club kept going after the campuses reopened, added a Slack forum for the between-meeting conversation, and grew a mailing list carrying seminar announcements across institutions that had never shared one.</p>
            </div>
          </li>

          <li>
            <div class="ncc-tl-year">2024</div>
            <div class="ncc-tl-body">
              <h3>First in-person symposium</h3>
              <p>Four years in, the club met in person for the first time — a full day of talks and posters, with the program built from submitted abstracts rather than an invitation list. That principle has held ever since.</p>
              <p><a href="/events/symposium-2024/">1st Annual Symposium &rarr;</a></p>
            </div>
          </li>

          <li>
            <div class="ncc-tl-year">2025</div>
            <div class="ncc-tl-body">
              <h3>Second symposium, at Einstein</h3>
              <p>The Bronx hosted, with keynotes from <strong>Danny Reinberg</strong> and <strong>Bing Ren</strong>. The host campus has rotated every year since, so no single institution owns the meeting.</p>
              <p><a href="/events/symposium-2025/">2nd Annual Symposium &rarr;</a></p>
            </div>
          </li>

          <li>
            <div class="ncc-tl-year">2026</div>
            <div class="ncc-tl-body">
              <h3>Third symposium, at Columbia</h3>
              <p>Washington Heights hosted, with keynotes from <strong>Brian Strahl</strong> and <strong>Emily Bernstein</strong>. By now the steering committee spans eight institutions across the city.</p>
              <p><a href="/events/symposium-2026/">3rd Annual Symposium &rarr;</a></p>
            </div>
          </li>

          <li class="ncc-tl-next">
            <div class="ncc-tl-year">2027</div>
            <div class="ncc-tl-body">
              <h3>Fourth symposium</h3>
              <p>Planned for summer 2027. The date, the host campus, and the abstract deadline go out on the mailing list first.</p>
              <p><a href="/join/">Join the mailing list &rarr;</a></p>
            </div>
          </li>

        </ol>
