The third annual symposium was held at the **Vagelos Education Center**, Columbia University Irving Medical Center, on Tuesday, July 21, 2026. It was free to attend, as always, and open to anyone in the New York area working on chromatin, epigenetics, or nuclear biology.

## Keynote speakers

**Brian Strahl, PhD** — Professor of Biochemistry and Biophysics, UNC School of Medicine. Co-author of the histone code hypothesis; his lab works on how chromatin modifications are written, read, and coupled to transcription.

**Emily Bernstein, PhD** — Professor of Oncological Sciences, Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, and a member of the club's steering committee. Her lab studies histone variants and chromatin regulation in cancer, with a focus on melanoma.

## Program

The rest of the program — the selected talks and the poster session — was drawn from **submitted abstracts**, with priority given to graduate students, postdocs, and new investigators. Lunch and refreshments were served.
