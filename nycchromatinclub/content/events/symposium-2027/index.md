The fourth annual symposium is planned for **summer 2027**. The date, the host campus, the keynote speakers and the abstract deadline all go out on the [mailing list](/join/) before they appear anywhere else.

{{< event-actions >}}

## What to expect

A full day, free at the door, on a rotating New York campus. Two keynote lectures, a program of selected talks, and a poster session over lunch. Talks and posters are chosen from **submitted abstracts**, with priority given to graduate students, postdocs, and new investigators — there is no invitation list to get onto.

## Agenda

The running order is published here once the program is set. For a sense of the shape of the day, see the [2026 agenda](/events/symposium-2026/).
