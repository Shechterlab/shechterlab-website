The second annual symposium was held at **Albert Einstein College of Medicine** on July 15, 2025.

## Keynote speakers

**Danny Reinberg, PhD** — on Polycomb repression, epigenetic inheritance, and the mechanisms of transcription.

**Bing Ren, PhD** — on three-dimensional genome organization, enhancer biology, and single-cell epigenomics.

The rest of the program was drawn from submitted abstracts, with talks and a poster session over lunch.
