      text: |
        Sponsorship pays for the room, the catering, the poster boards, and trainee travel. In return sponsors get signage, acknowledgement in the program and from the podium, and — the part most sponsors actually care about — a table beside the poster session, where the whole meeting spends its longest unstructured block of the day.

        The audience is roughly the eight institutions on the [steering committee](/about/#steering-committee) plus anyone else in the New York area doing chromatin work: principal investigators, postdocs, graduate students, and core facility staff, weighted toward people who choose reagents and instruments themselves.

        Three tiers, described on this page as **Lead**, **Supporting**, and **Exhibitor**. [Email the organizers](mailto:info@nycchromatinclub.org?subject=NYC%20Chromatin%20Club%20sponsorship) and the committee will send the current figures and what each tier includes.
