        - Chromatin assembly, histone biology, and epigenetics
        - RNA processing, splicing, and RNA-binding protein regulation
        - Chemical biology and small-molecule pharmacology
        - Cancer biology and translational mechanism
        - One-carbon metabolism and the methyl economy
        - Quantitative and computational analysis of gene regulation

  - block: markdown
    content:
      title: 'For postdoctoral applicants'
      text: |
        Strong postdoctoral applicants should bring a clear mechanistic skill set and a serious interest in connecting molecular detail to larger biological systems. Relevant backgrounds include chromatin biology, RNA processing, PTM enzymology, structural biology, proteomics, mass spectrometry, leukemia biology, or one-carbon metabolism.

        Active project areas open to postdocs include:

        - PRMT5-dependent regulation of RNA processing and the GRIPP chromatin reserve
        - PRMT5-H4 methylation and the demand side of the methyl economy
        - GNMT and the supply side of the methyl economy
        - TTLL4 glutamyltransferase biology in NPM1-mutant AML
        - PTM control of acidic and intrinsically disordered nuclear proteins
        - PRMT biology in ALS
