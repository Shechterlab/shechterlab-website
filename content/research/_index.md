title: Research
---

We study how chemical modifications of nuclear proteins control chromatin and RNA — and what goes wrong when that control fails in ALS, leukemia, and aging. The work is organized around two molecular research areas (methylation in gene regulation and RNA processing; glutamylation and histone chaperones) and three disease programs that grew directly out of them.

For a history of how these directions developed, see [Lab History →](/history).
