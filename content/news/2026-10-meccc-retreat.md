David gave a talk, "Targeting the TTLL4 Glutamyltransferase in NPM1c-Mutant AML," at the Montefiore Einstein Comprehensive Cancer Center Annual Scientific Retreat, *From Discovery to Impact*, held at Einstein on October 8.

The work is a shared project with [Kira Gritsman](https://einsteinmed.edu/faculty/14128/kira-gritsman) and [Seiya Kitamura](https://einsteinmed.edu/faculty/17244/seiya-kitamura), bringing together leukemia biology, medicinal chemistry, and the lab's enzymology. Much of what was presented comes from [Humaira Ilyas](/authors/humaira-ilyas/), a postdoc in the lab from 2021 to 2025.
