Roth et al. is under invited revision at *Nature Communications*. The work shows that PRMT5-dependent symmetric dimethylation of H4R3 is required for replication-dependent histone gene expression and genome integrity during S phase. An accompanying News and Views appears in *Nature Structural and Molecular Biology*.
