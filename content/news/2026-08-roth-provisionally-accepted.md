[Jacob Roth](/authors/jacob-roth/) and colleagues' paper on [PRMT5-dependent histone production](/publications/prmt5-histone-production-2025/) is now provisionally accepted at *Nature Communications*, following [invited revision](/news/2026-04-roth-revision/) earlier this year. The paper shows that PRMT5-dependent symmetric dimethylation of H4R3 is required for replication-dependent histone gene expression and genome integrity during S phase, and is accompanied by a News and Views in *Nature Structural and Molecular Biology*.
