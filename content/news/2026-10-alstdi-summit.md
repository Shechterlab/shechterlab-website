David gave an invited talk, "PRMTs in ALS: From RNA Regulation to Therapeutic Opportunity," at the [ALS-TDI Research Summit](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLV9fTeqbMjNQ) — about ALS as a disease of central dogma stress, and how PRMTs and methylarginine may regulate RNA biology to help retune and treat both sporadic and C9orf72-mutant ALS. [Aliza Silverstein](/authors/aliza-silverstein/) presented her work on methylarginine regulation of C9orf72 dipeptide-repeat protein toxicity.

[Watch David's talk →](https://youtu.be/wLYqduaxEvE?si=vn9V1D7ykB5OQCyA) &ensp;·&ensp; [Full summit playlist →](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLV9fTeqbMjNQ)

ALS-TDI's [Key Insights from the 2026 ALS TDI Summit](https://www.als.net/news/key-insights-from-the-2026-als-tdi-summit/) rounds up the meeting, with a summary of David's talk among them.

The [ALS Therapy Development Institute](https://www.als.net) is the lab's primary partner on C9orf72 ALS.
