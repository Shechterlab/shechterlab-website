Isaac Kraz and collaborators' paper on the GNMT N-terminus is now published in the [Journal of Biological Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2026.113468) ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021925826023409)). The paper establishes the GNMT N-terminal domain — and S9 phosphorylation specifically — as a folate-responsive rheostat that couples folate cycle flux to cellular methylation potential, framing GNMT as the supply-side regulator in our methyl economy model. First posted as a [bioRxiv preprint](/news/2026-05-gnmt-preprint/) in May.
