abstract: "Methylation of arginine residues by protein arginine methyltransferases (PRMTs) is an underexplored component of the histone code of epigenetic regulation. The histone code encompasses an exceedingly complex combination of post-translational modifications (PTMs) of histone residues that marks and directs utilization of the underlying genome. We created a peptide array containing all core, variant and linker histone primary amino acid sequences and all known and possible post-translational modifications, alone and in combination. Here, we utilized these arrays to study the substrate specificity of a critical enzyme complex."
